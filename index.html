How I animate

1

Storyboard

Thumbnails and camera plans define the story beats before a single frame is drawn.

2

Keyframes

Strong poses first. Timing and spacing are set here, which is where the energy comes from.

3

Inbetweens

Smooth, weighty motion with squash, stretch and smears where the action needs them.

4

Polish

Colour, lighting, sound and effects, then review until it feels alive.
